# reserve

> [!summary] 一眼记住
> **/rɪˈzɜːrv/** · *n., v.* · 预订；保留；储备
> 遇见形式：“reserve” → 原形，具体词性和义项待语境确认

## 中文释义

- **动词**：预订座位、房间等；保留某物供特定用途。
- **名词**：备用的人、物或资金；自然保护区等受保护区域。

## Explain it in English

> To reserve something is to keep it for a particular person, time, or purpose. A reserve is an extra supply kept for future need.

## 形态、词族与搭配

- **变化**：reserves；reserved；reserving。
- **词族**：reservation（预订；保留意见）；reserved（预留的；矜持的）。
- **常见搭配**：reserve a room；reserve the right to；in reserve；nature reserve；energy reserve。
