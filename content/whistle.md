## 中文释义

- 口哨声；哨子
- 吹口哨；发出尖锐的哨声

## Explain it in English

> A whistle is a clear, high sound made by blowing air, or a small device that makes that sound. To whistle is to produce it.

## 形态、词族与搭配

- **变化**：whistles · whistled · whistling
- **词族**：whistler（吹口哨的人）；whistling（呼啸的；吹哨）
- **常见搭配**：blow a whistle, whistle loudly, final whistle, whistle for attention

## 例句

- **原创例句 1**：The referee blew the whistle as soon as the player crossed the line.
  中文：球员越过界线后，裁判立刻吹响了哨子。
- **原创例句 2**：Wind whistled through the narrow gap in the window.
  中文：风从窗户的窄缝中呼啸而过。

## 学习与应用

- **阅读识别**：根据 referee、coach 判断哨子；根据 wind、kettle、air 判断尖锐声。
- **写作或口语**：可描述体育信号、人的动作或风和机械产生的高音。
- **易错点**：whistle 中的 t 不发音；动词过去式是 whistled。
